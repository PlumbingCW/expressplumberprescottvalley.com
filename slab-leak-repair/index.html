Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Prescott Valley, AZ

A slab leak is a break in a water line under or inside a concrete foundation. The signs are a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water with everything off, wet carpet from below and a bill that jumps with no change in habits.

If you suspect one, shut off the main water valve and call our 24/7 line. We locate the leak first, then explain whether a spot repair, a reroute or a repipe fits the house.

Why it is urgent

A slab leak is an emergency

Water under a slab erodes the soil that supports the concrete. It can lift or crack the slab, soak the floor and raise the risk of mold. Even a small leak keeps running and adds to your bill.

Close the main valve and call the 24/7 line. Do not break into the floor yourself. Water and electrical circuits mix in ways that are hard to see.

Signs

How to recognize a slab leak

A warm patch on the floor

A hot water line is leaking under the slab and heating the concrete above it.

A meter that keeps moving

Water use continues with every fixture off.

The sound of running water

A hiss or trickle with nothing running.

Damp or lifted flooring

Moisture rises through concrete and shows up in carpet or tile.

Process

How we find and fix it

1

A meter and pressure test shows the line is losing water.

2

Acoustic and thermal tools locate the leak under the slab.

3

We compare spot repair, a reroute through walls or attic and a full repipe.

4

Fix and test

The line is repaired or replaced, pressure-tested and the floor is patched.

Spot repair under the slab

  • Opens a small hole in the concrete
  • Quickest for a single failure
  • Does not address other worn lines
  • Lower cost for a one-time fix

Reroute or repipe

  • Runs new lines in walls or the attic, away from the slab
  • Avoids concrete cutting
  • Replaces suspect pipe
  • Best when pipes are worn or leaks repeat
Causes

Why slab leaks happen

Copper pipe can develop pinholes from corrosion, abrasion or poor water chemistry. Pipes rub against concrete as the house expands and contracts with temperature. Soil movement can stress a line.

Ask your water utility for its annual water quality report, or test your tap, if you want to learn more about the water in your lines. Water chemistry plays a role in how fast metal pipe ages.

Not every Prescott Valley home has a slab foundation. Some homes are built over crawlspaces, and in those homes we look under the floor instead. A technician confirms the foundation type on the first visit.

Codes and standards

Copper water tube for building supply is made to ASTM B88, and pipe in contact with drinking water should carry NSF/ANSI 61 certification. Both are published standards that apply to any repair or reroute we price.
