Information we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name, your address and a description of the problem. We use that to book and complete your service.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call, you agree to that recording.

This website

This site does not contain forms. It does not ask for personal details beyond what you choose to tell us by phone or email. If you email us, we read your message and the address you used to reply.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps from Google Maps. When your browser loads those, the provider may receive your IP address and device details under its own policy.

How we use information

  • To answer your call, schedule service and send a plumber
  • To improve quality and train staff
  • To respond to your email
  • To meet legal and insurance requirements

Sharing

We share your name, address and phone number with the licensed plumber assigned to your job so they can reach you. We may share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing contact@expressplumberprescottvalley.com. You can ask the dispatcher not to record a call, and we will note it where we can.
