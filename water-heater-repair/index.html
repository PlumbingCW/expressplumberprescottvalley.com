Urgent signs

When a water heater failure is an emergency

Not every water heater problem is urgent. Lukewarm water can wait until morning, but a few symptoms should not. Water pooling around the base, a hot smell near a gas unit, steam from the relief valve pipe and a tank that has gone cold in freezing weather all deserve a same-day call.

Prescott Valley sits at about 5,100 feet, and the January average low is around 26 degrees Fahrenheit. A garage or exterior closet can drop below freezing overnight, so a heater in an unheated space has less margin than one in a Phoenix utility room.

Do these while you wait for the plumber

  • ✓Shut the cold water supply valve on the pipe entering the top of the tank.
  • ✓Turn the gas control knob to off, or switch off the 240 volt breaker for an electric unit.
  • ✓Keep people and pets away from the relief valve discharge pipe, because that water can be scalding.
  • ✓Move boxes and stored items off the floor around the tank.
  • ✓Do not drain the tank unless a plumber tells you to, since a hot tank can burn.
Diagnosis

Why there is no hot water

Most no-hot-water calls trace to a short list of failed parts. On a gas heater that is usually the thermocouple, the gas control valve or a dirty pilot assembly. On an electric heater it is a tripped high-limit switch, a burned-out heating element or a failed thermostat.

A technician tests each part with a multimeter or a manometer instead of guessing. Replacing a heating element or thermocouple is a repair priced by the part and the labor. A tank with a failed inner shell is a different story, and we say so before any work starts.

Tank types

Gas, electric and hybrid units

Gas heaters need a clear flue, correct combustion air and a gas line that passes a leak test. Electric heaters need a healthy 240 volt circuit and a tight set of element gaskets. Hybrid heat pump units add a compressor and a condensate line, so they have a few more places to fail.

We repair all three types. For a hybrid unit that is still under its manufacturer warranty, we look at the warranty terms before touching a part, because the wrong repair can void coverage.

Codes and standards

Gas storage water heaters are built to ANSI Z21.10.1 and electric tank heaters are listed to UL 174. Relief valves follow ANSI Z21.22. We read the maker manual first, because repairs that ignore it can void a warranty.

Decision

Repair or replace the tank

A good rule is to compare the repair bill to the cost of a new unit. If the tank is under eight years old and the failure is a part, repair is almost always right. If the tank is past ten years and leaking, the shell has failed and a repair will not hold.

When replacement wins, our water heater replacement service handles the swap, usually the same day. If you want to move to a gas-saving design, ask about a tankless water heater while the plumber is already on site.
