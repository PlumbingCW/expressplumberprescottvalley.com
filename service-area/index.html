Home base

Prescott Valley and its ZIP codes

Prescott Valley is our home base, and it is the largest town we serve. It counted 46,785 residents in the 2020 census and sits at about 5,100 feet along State Route 69. We cover ZIP codes 86312, 86314 and 86315.

Landmarks that help us orient on a call include Glassford Hill, Fain Park, the Findlay Toyota Center, Glassford Hill Marketplace and the Yavapai Regional Medical Center East Campus. If you tell the dispatcher which one is nearest, a plumber can find your street faster.

East of town

Dewey-Humboldt

Dewey-Humboldt incorporated on December 20, 2004, and spans ZIP codes 86329 and 86327. It sits in the Agua Fria River valley along State Route 69. Lots are larger and some properties may be on septic or private wells, so we ask about that on the call. Our Dewey-Humboldt page has the details.

North of town

Chino Valley

Chino Valley uses ZIP code 86323 and sits at about 4,750 feet on State Route 89. Winter nights run colder than in Prescott Valley, so frozen and burst lines are common calls. Our Chino Valley page covers what to do before we arrive.

What we do

Services across the area

Every service listed on our services page is available across the area. Emergency calls such as burst pipes, sewer backups and water heater failures are the most common. Planned work such as water heater installs and repipes is scheduled for normal hours.

Good directions

What to tell the dispatcher about your address

The more precisely you can describe where you are, the faster a plumber arrives. Give the street address, the nearest cross street and the nearest landmark. In Prescott Valley, a major road such as State Route 69 or a landmark such as Glassford Hill is a good anchor.

In Dewey-Humboldt and Chino Valley, many homes sit on rural roads that GPS maps handle poorly. Tell the dispatcher which highway you are closest to, and whether there is a gate or a long driveway. A call that mentions a locked gate or a dog saves a trip.

Details that speed up dispatch

  • ✓The full street address and ZIP code
  • ✓The nearest cross street or landmark
  • ✓Whether the property has a gate, a long drive or a dog
  • ✓Whether you are on town water or a private well
  • ✓Whether the home uses a sewer connection or a septic system
What changes the response

Weather, distance and timing

Winter storms, icy mornings and road closures can slow every dispatch in the area. A snow or ice event also raises call volume, since frozen pipes and failed heaters peak together. The dispatcher tells you what is realistic for your address and keeps you updated if that changes.

Prescott Valley sits at about 5,100 feet, Chino Valley at about 4,750 feet and Dewey-Humboldt at about 4,580 feet, so conditions differ from town to town even on the same night.

Coverage rules

How we decide what is in our area

Dispatch looks at the address, the type of problem and the time of day. Close addresses get same-day appointments when available. Farther addresses may take longer, and we say so before dispatch.

Not every nearby community has its own page. Small communities near the three towns above are served from the same line, so call and ask.
