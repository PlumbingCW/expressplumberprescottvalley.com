The short version

What we do

When you call, a dispatcher asks about the problem, the address and how urgent it is. We tell you the first steps, quote a clear price and send a licensed plumber. The goal is simple: a plumber who can fix the problem, at a price you know before work begins.

We cover water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, fixtures and new plumbing. Our services page lists everything.

Honest limits

What we do not do

We do not promise a specific arrival time, because traffic, weather and the number of calls change by the hour. We do not give prices for a job we have not heard about. We do not handle large mechanical systems or specialty trades, and we tell you when a job is beyond our scope.

Verify

Check any plumber, including us

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors publishes a Contractor Search at azroc.gov. Use it to confirm a contractor holds a license that covers the work, check the contact number on the record and ask for written estimates. We encourage every homeowner to do that before signing anything.

From call to repair

How a typical call goes

1

You call the 24/7 line

A dispatcher answers and asks what is happening, where you are and how urgent it is.

2

We give first steps

You hear what to shut off and what to avoid while a plumber is on the way.

3

You hear a clear price

We quote before work starts. After-hours rates are given on the call.

4

A plumber arrives and fixes it

The plumber diagnoses the problem, does the work, tests it and explains what was done.

The dispatcher is not a salesperson. The job is to get the right plumber to the right address with the right information, so the visit goes smoothly.

If the problem turns out to be smaller than you feared, we tell you. If it is bigger, we explain the options and let you decide.

Scope

Services we cover and services we refer

We cover water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, burst pipes, fixtures, disposals, repiping, new installs, small commercial work and water treatment. The full list is on the services page.

A few jobs belong to other trades. Well drilling and pump replacement, large mechanical systems, fire sprinkler work and pool shell leaks are specialties. When one of those is the real problem, we say so and point you in the right direction.

Hours are simple: the line never closes. Nights, weekends and holidays get the same dispatch as a Tuesday morning. What changes after hours is price and availability, and we tell you both on the call before a plumber is sent.

How we talk to you

Our approach to honest advice

  • We say when a repair is not worth it, such as a leaking tank shell
  • We explain what insurance usually covers and what it often does not
  • We tell you what you can safely do yourself and when to stop
  • We give a price before work starts and tell you what could change it
  • We do not claim awards, ratings or credentials we cannot show

That last point matters. This site does not list reviews, ratings or certifications, because we would rather show you how to check a plumber than ask you to trust a badge.

Why local matters

Local notes we use

Prescott Valley sits at about 5,100 feet, with January lows around 26 degrees Fahrenheit. That shapes the plumbing: exposed pipes freeze, garage water heaters work harder and outdoor bibs split. Our guides cover those local issues. See our plumbing guides for examples.

We also cover Dewey-Humboldt and Chino Valley, where lots are larger and nights are colder.
