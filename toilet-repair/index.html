Urgent steps

Toilet overflow: stop it first

  1. Lift the tank lid and press the rubber flapper down to stop water entering the bowl.
  2. Close the shutoff valve on the wall behind the toilet by turning it clockwise.
  3. Lay towels around the base and keep people out of wet areas.
  4. Do not flush again, and do not use chemical drain cleaner.
  5. Call the 24/7 line if the toilet is backing up or the water is not stopping.
Constant water

Running toilet

A toilet that keeps running wastes water all day. The usual causes are a worn flapper, a flapper chain that is too tight, a fill valve that no longer shuts off and a crack in the overflow tube.

Replacing the flapper and fill valve fixes most running toilets. If the tank has a crack or the bowl rocks, a new toilet may be the cleaner answer.

Leaks

Leaks at the base or the tank

Water around the base points to a failed wax ring or loose closet bolts. A leak between the tank and bowl comes from worn tank bolts and gaskets. A drip from the supply line comes from a worn connector or a stiff shutoff valve.

Wet subfloor under a leaking toilet is common, so we check the floor for soft spots while the toilet is up.

Install

Installing a new toilet

A new toilet is a straightforward job when the flange and shutoff valve are sound. We remove the old toilet, check and repair the flange, set the new one on a fresh wax ring, connect a new supply line and test the flush.

If you have a hardware question, such as a taller bowl or a dual flush model, ask when you call. We help you choose one that fits your bathroom.

Escalation

When toilet repairs are not enough

A toilet that will not clear even after plunging and that backs up other fixtures points to a drain or main line problem. That is a job for drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning, not for a new flapper.

Codes and standards

Toilets with the EPA WaterSense label use 1.28 gallons or less per flush, and ceramic fixtures are made to ASME A112.19.2. When we replace a toilet, we match the rough-in dimension and check the flange to the maker instructions.
