What to do in the first five minutes

  • ✓Close the main water shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house.
  • ✓Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain remaining water.
  • ✓Switch off power to the affected area if it is safe to reach the breaker.
  • ✓Move electronics, rugs and valuables out of the water.
  • ✓Take photos and video of the damage before cleanup, then call the 24/7 line.

Do not wait for the water to stop on its own. A split pipe keeps flowing until the supply is closed, and every minute raises the cleanup bill.

Local cold

Why pipes burst in a high-desert town

Prescott Valley sits at about 5,100 feet, and the January average low is about 26 degrees Fahrenheit. Pipes in exterior walls, garages, crawlspaces and attics can freeze on the coldest nights. Ice expands in the pipe and the split often shows up only after the thaw.

Insulating exposed lines and letting a faucet drip on hard-freeze nights are simple steps, and we can add insulation during a repair.

Process

Repair, then protect the rest of the line

A burst pipe tells us that the system was under stress. We look at the rest of the run for weak spots, check pressure at the house and recommend a pressure regulator if the supply pressure is too high.

Where several pipes have failed, repiping may be more economical than patching one at a time.

Dry-out and restoration are separate jobs. We stop the water and repair the pipe, and a restoration contractor handles drying and mold prevention. Your insurer can point you to one, or we can suggest what to ask for.

Codes and standards

Model plumbing codes such as the IPC call for water pipes to be protected from freezing when they run in exterior walls, attics or crawlspaces. Repairs we make follow that principle by adding insulation where the layout allows.
