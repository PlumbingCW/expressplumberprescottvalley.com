Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Prescott Valley, AZ

When the main sewer line clogs, every drain in the house feels it. Water backs into the lowest fixture, usually a tub or a floor drain, and it does not stop until the line is cleared.

Stop using water, keep the family out of the wet area and call our 24/7 line. A plumber can clear the main at the cleanout and then check what caused it.

Urgent steps

Sewer backup: what to do first

  1. Stop using water in the house, including toilets, laundry and the dishwasher.
  2. Keep people and pets away from the backed-up fixture.
  3. Look for the main cleanout and leave it capped until the plumber arrives.
  4. Photograph any water damage for your records.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are affected.

Wastewater in a living space is a health hazard. Cleaning it up is the job of a restoration crew, and the plumber is there to clear the cause so the line can drain again.

Causes

Common causes of a main line clog

Roots enter at joints and grow into a mat that catches paper. Grease builds up on the pipe wall. Flushed wipes labelled flushable do not break down like toilet paper. A low spot, called a belly, holds water and solids.

In Prescott Valley most homes are newer plastic lines, so roots and wipes are common culprits. Properties out in Dewey-Humboldt and Chino Valley can be on septic systems, which have their own service needs and which we scope before cleaning.

Codes and standards

Model plumbing codes such as the UPC and the IPC set minimum slopes and require cleanouts on building sewers. A sagging section, called a belly, is a slope problem that a cleaning cannot fix.
