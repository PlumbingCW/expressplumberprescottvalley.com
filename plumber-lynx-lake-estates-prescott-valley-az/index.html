What we know about Lynx Lake Estates

Real-estate guides place the neighborhood on the southern edge of Prescott Valley, bordering State Route 69 and close to the Town Center, with listings in ZIP code 86314. They describe ranch-style homes in a range of ages and sizes, with homes dating from the 1960s up to recent builds.

Some listings mention lots of about half an acre or more, and local guides mention gravel driveways and no sidewalks on some streets. Prescott Valley Inc. began selling home lots in 1966, so the earliest homes here belong to the first years of the town.

Practical guidance

Mixed ages, mixed plumbing

We never assume what is inside a house from its age. A 1970s home may have been repiped, and a newer home can have an odd repair history. What we do is ask questions, look at what is visible and recommend an inspection where it will answer something real.

On larger lots the line from the meter to the house is simply longer, and the house may sit farther from the street. That is a reason to know where your main shutoff and your cleanout are, and to find out whether the home is on sewer or on a septic system, which an address-level check with the utility or county can confirm.

Know what you have

When an inspection beats a guess

If you have owned an older home for years and are not sure what is under the floor or the yard, a camera inspection shows the pipe and its condition. It turns an assumption into a picture and can save a wrong repair.

If an inspection or a repeated failure does show worn pipe, repiping and sewer line repair are the conversations to have, with the camera footage in hand.

Before you call

  • ✓Say whether the house is on sewer or septic if you know.
