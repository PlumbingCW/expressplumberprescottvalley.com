The area

What we know about StoneRidge

Town and local sources agree on the basics. StoneRidge sits on the west side of Prescott Valley, with its entrance off State Route 69, and Fain Park lies south of the highway and east of Stoneridge Drive. The golf course was designed by Randy Heckenkemper and opened in 2002, which gives a rough start date for much of the community.

Listing sites put the typical build year in the mid-2000s and describe a spread of single-story homes, two-story homes and larger custom houses, with some townhomes. We treat those figures as a guide and not as a survey. The homes you are calling about may be older or newer than the average.

Practical guidance

Water heaters in homes from the 2000s

Tank water heaters are commonly cited as lasting somewhere around 8 to 12 years, depending on the model and the water. A home built in the mid-2000s may be on its first, second or even third heater, so the date on the label matters more than the age of the house.

Signs that a heater is near the end include rust-colored hot water, pooling around the base, a rumbling sound from sediment and repeated pilot or element problems. A leak from the tank itself usually means replacement. A leak from a fitting or valve may be a repair.

Outdoors

Outdoor lines and the golf course edge

Many StoneRidge lots back onto open space or the fairways. Outdoor hose bibs and irrigation connections are exposed to the same winter lows as the rest of Prescott Valley, which average roughly 25 to 28 degrees from December through February. Drain and disconnect before the first hard freeze.

If a spigot drips or the line to it has split, water line repair covers the run from the meter to the house, and faucet repair covers the fixture itself.
