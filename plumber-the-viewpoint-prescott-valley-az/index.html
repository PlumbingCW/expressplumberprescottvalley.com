What we know about The Viewpoint

A regional community directory describes The Viewpoint as a master-planned community with a homeowners association, north of State Route 89A on Viewpoint Drive. It lists four parts, the original Viewpoint plus East, West and North, and says the largest, Viewpoint North, is expected to exceed 1,100 homes at build-out.

Real-estate guides describe traditional, ranch and Mediterranean-style homes on quarter-acre lots. We have no boundary map for the individual sections, so we describe the community by its anchor road and ask for your street when you call.

Practical guidance

Larger homes and hot water demand

A home with several bathrooms and a big kitchen can ask a lot of one water heater. If two showers and a dishwasher run together and the water turns cold, the heater may be undersized for the load, or it may be failing. The two look alike from the shower.

We do not assume which it is. A plumber can check the size and age of the unit, the temperature setting and the distance to the farthest bathroom, then tell you whether a repair, a bigger tank or a second heater makes sense.

Layout notes

Hillside lots and bathrooms upstairs

Homes built to take in a mountain view often have bathrooms on more than one level. A leak on an upper floor can show up as a stain on the ceiling below long after it started, so a small stain deserves quick attention.

If you are planning a remodel, a new bathroom or a laundry move, plumbing installation begins with where supply and drain lines can run. For hot water on demand across a large home, a tankless water heater is a conversation about flow and gas or electric capacity.

Before you call about a ceiling stain

  • ✓Find the bathroom or appliance above the stain and close its shutoff valve.
  • ✓Place a bucket and photograph the stain.
  • ✓Do not poke the drywall if it is bulging, since it may hold water.
  • ✓Note whether the stain grows when the fixture is used.
