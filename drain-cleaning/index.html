Urgent signs

When a clogged drain is an emergency

A single slow sink can wait for a normal appointment. A drain that is backing up into a tub or a floor drain cannot. Water from a main line backup is dirty, and every hour on flooring raises cleanup cost.

Call right away if more than one fixture is affected, if water or sewage is rising in a low drain, or if you smell sewer gas inside the house.

While you wait for us

  • ✓Stop running water, laundry and the dishwasher.
  • ✓Do not pour chemical drain cleaner, because it can burn the plumber and damage pipes.
  • ✓Keep children and pets out of any wet area.
  • ✓Locate the main cleanout if you can, usually a capped pipe outside near the foundation.
  • ✓Take photos of any water damage for your records.
Kitchen

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog with grease, coffee grounds and food scraps. Grease cools and coats the pipe wall, and the opening shrinks until water barely moves. A cable with a cutting head clears the soft buildup, and for heavy grease we may suggest hydro jetting.

If the garbage disposal is involved, see our garbage disposal repair page, since a jammed unit and a clogged trap are different problems.

Bathrooms

Tub, shower and bathroom sink drains

Hair, soap and mineral buildup hold most bathroom clogs. A drain snake pulls the mass out in one pass in most cases. We remove and clean the stopper or strainer assembly as part of the job when it helps.

A shower that drains slowly again a few weeks later points to a deeper clog or a belly in the pipe, and a camera answers that.

Hidden leak

A drain line that leaks

A drain pipe can leak as well as clog, and the signs are damp drywall, a musty smell under a sink or a stain under a bathroom. If we suspect a leaking drain line, we camera the line and point to the exact spot, which may lead to a repair rather than another cleaning.

Codes and standards

Both the UPC and the IPC require traps and vents on fixture drains, and they call for cleanouts at set points on a drain system. That is why a missing cleanout can turn a simple clog into a bigger job.
